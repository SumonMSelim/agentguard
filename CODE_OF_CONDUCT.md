# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We are committed to making participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our Standards

**Expected behaviour:**
- Use welcoming and inclusive language
- Respect differing viewpoints and experiences
- Accept constructive criticism gracefully
- Focus on what is best for the community
- Show empathy toward other community members

**Unacceptable behaviour:**
- Harassment, insults, or derogatory comments in any form
- Publishing others' private information without explicit permission
- Trolling or sustained disruptive behaviour
- Any conduct that would be considered inappropriate in a professional setting

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in all project spaces — GitHub issues, pull requests, discussions, and any other venue where you represent this project.

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behaviour may be reported by opening a [GitHub issue](https://github.com/SumonMSelim/agentguard/issues) marked **[conduct]** or by contacting the maintainers directly. All complaints will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly. Maintainers are obligated to maintain confidentiality with regard to the reporter.

Violations may result in a temporary or permanent ban from the project.
